# Visage Source Material Pool

Place your authentic real-world photos here. These serve as the foundational training and reference source pool for creating varied, highly accurate, and evolving persona renditions over time.

---

## 📸 What to Drop Here

To prevent "clone syndrome" and ensure AI-generated personas genuinely capture your real essence across different dimensions:

- **Recent iPhone Photos**: Export full-resolution uncompressed selfies, portraits, and candid shots.
- **Amazon Photos / Google Cloud / iCloud Archives**: Photos across recent months/years showing natural shifts in styling, facial hair, eyewear, and hats.
- **Varied Lighting & Environments**: Outdoor natural light, indoor workspace, evening gatherings, high-contrast studio lights.
- **Multiple Angles & Expressions**: Forward-facing, 45-degree angle, side profile, broad smile, focused/contemplative gaze.

---

## ⚙️ How the Multi-Source Generator Works

When the automated generator script runs:

1. It randomly or sequentially samples **3 to 5 real photos** from this directory.
2. It blends facial geometry, skin tones, and distinctive features with the target persona's concept.
3. As you add more photos to this pool over the coming weeks and months, new iterations naturally mirror your authentic, evolving visage.

---

## 📁 Supported Formats

- `.jpg` / `.jpeg`
- `.png`
- `.webp`
- `.heic` _(recommended to export as high-quality JPG/PNG)_
